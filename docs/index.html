Margin notes

    Optional rule-based grammar layer from the Harper project. Uses a local copy in docs/vendor if present, otherwise downloads about 27 MB from jsDelivr.

    How it works

    Spelling. Every dictionary word becomes a 1,024-bit hypervector: each character bigram and trigram of #word# is bound by XOR with position encoded by rotation, and the n-grams are bundled by majority vote. A second hypervector does the same for the word's Double Metaphone code, so ressapee lands near recipe. An unknown word is matched against all 80,000 words by Hamming distance, then reranked by frequency and edit distance.

    Word choice. For each of 320 confusion sets (form/from, their/there, quite/quiet …) every member has a class hypervector learned by random indexing from about 7.7 million words: the four words on each side are bundled in, each permuted by its position. Mistakes during training are corrected by moving the context out of the wrong class and into the right one. A word is flagged only when another member fits its sentence clearly better. Careful uses the threshold that kept false marks near 1.5% on held-out text; Balanced and Eager halve and quarter it, catching more slips at the cost of more false marks.
